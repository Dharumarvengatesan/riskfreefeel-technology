The Founder's Story

"I Founded RiskFreeFeel
Because Clients Deserve Better."

Hi, I'm Dharumar Vengatesan — developer, entrepreneur, and founder of RiskFreeFeel Technologies. I started this company in 2024 with a very personal mission: to give every business — whether a first-time startup founder or an established brand — access to the same quality of software that big corporations pay millions for.

After working with clients who had been burned by agencies that overpromised and underdelivered — missed deadlines, bloated costs, poor communication — I knew something had to change.
