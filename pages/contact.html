FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a mobile app? +
A typical mobile app takes 8-16 weeks depending on complexity. Simple apps: 6-8 weeks. Mid-complexity: 10-14 weeks. Enterprise-grade: 16-24 weeks. We always provide a detailed timeline after discovery.
What is your pricing model? +
We offer fixed-price packages for defined projects and time-&-material billing for ongoing work. Payment is milestone-based — you only pay when you approve each deliverable. View our Pricing page for detailed plans.
Do you provide source code ownership? +
Absolutely. 100% of the source code belongs to you upon final payment. We transfer all code, assets, and documentation. No vendor lock-in, ever. You get full intellectual property rights.
Do you sign NDAs? +
Yes, we sign NDA agreements before any sensitive discussions. Your business ideas and data are fully protected. We take confidentiality extremely seriously and have a clean track record in this regard.
What post-launch support do you provide? +
All projects include 6 months of free maintenance — bug fixes, security patches, and minor updates. After that, we offer flexible support retainer plans. Our 24/7 monitoring ensures your product is always online.
